Case study — product build

Stockify: a multi-tenant inventory SaaS, built end to end

A stock management platform for small businesses, starting with a real client — a motorcycle parts shop — and designed from day one to scale to other verticals. I own the full stack: data model, API, frontend, auth, and the AI features on top.

ASP.NET Core SQL Server React + TypeScript JWT / Multi-tenant AI Assistant AFIP e-invoicing

Dashboard — live KPIs, monthly margin calculated from real per-sale costs, top products and best customers, 30-day sales chart.

What it does

Inventory & POS

Products, categories, suppliers, stock entries and a fast counter-sale ("Modo caja") flow, with barcode scanner support.

Multi-tenant from day one

Every business gets its own isolated data behind a single deployment — JWT claims carry the tenant ID, every query is scoped by it.

AI stock assistant

Natural-language questions about inventory ("what's running low?", "what should I reorder?") answered against the business's real data.

Argentine e-invoicing

AFIP/ARCA electronic invoicing integration, so sales generate compliant fiscal documents, not just internal records.

Quotes & running accounts

Customer quotes ("Presupuestos") and running credit accounts ("Cuenta corriente") for repeat business customers.

Built for real usage

Designed around an actual shop's workflow — a business that used to track stock by memory, now with alerts before it runs out.

Product catalog — cost/price/margin per item, live stock status, low-stock flagging.

AI assistant — the interface layer over a provider-agnostic IAiService, currently backed by Llama 3.3 via Groq.
